# A missing fact becomes a card, not a ping

The one-pager sends a ping when files disagree. A ping is an interruption in someone else's tool, and the person still has to reconstruct the answer. Index instead shows that person one card: the question, a draft, and the passages. They confirm, correct, or hand it to the other person named on the card. The confirmed sentence is what the next ask gets. Files stay as evidence you can open from the answer. They are not a place you go to browse.
