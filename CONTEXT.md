# Index

A way for someone to get a fact that the files do not settle, by asking the person who knows.

## Language

**Ask**:
A question from one person about a fact the files do not settle.
_Avoid_: Ping, query, search

**Card**:
The one question, drafted answer, and passages a person receives. They can confirm it, correct it, or pass it on.
_Avoid_: Ping, notification, message, thread

**Evidence**:
A passage Index read. Opening it shows that passage and who owns it.
_Avoid_: File browser, drive, library

**Known answer**:
A sentence someone confirmed or corrected, which Index uses the next time the question comes up.
_Avoid_: Memory, document, page

**Workstream**:
A body of work a person is on, or is watching, or that sits near their work.
_Avoid_: Project folder, workspace

**Overlap**:
Two workstreams whose plans disagree. It is settled by a card, not by a meeting.
_Avoid_: Conflict ticket, alert
